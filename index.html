    About Lahore Rush

    The upgraded sequel to Rikshaw Rush: an endless 3D runner on a 125 or an auto-rickshaw through 37 real places of Lahore — from Delhi Gate and Gawalmandi to Anarkali, Minar-e-Pakistan, Canal Road and MM Alam Road. No place repeats until you've seen them all.

    Engineering highlights
    • Blender-built hero models. The 125 (spoked wheels, finned engine, crash guard, saree guard, rider in shalwar kameez), tonga, bhains, Minar-e-Pakistan and Badshahi Masjid are generated by a Python script in Blender and exported as glTF; the garage recolours their materials live.
    • Synthesised 4-stroke engine. Every combustion pulse is scheduled in real time at rpm/120, so the exhaust note keeps its natural tone from the 11 Hz idle "dug-dug" to the 70 Hz top-gear "brrraap". It has a 4-speed box with throttle cut, clutch dip, gear clunk and downshift blip, plus kick-start, silencer crackle, wind, tyre and chain noise. No audio files.
    • Two rides. A leaning 125 with wheelies, or an auto-rickshaw with soft springs, a raspy 2-stroke engine and a two-wheel "do pahiya" stunt.
    • Built to run smooth on phones. Blender models are merged at load into a few draw calls, the curved world is culled on the CPU, every shader is compiled behind the loading screen, and the render scale adapts to hold 60 fps.
    • Endless, never-repeating route. Places are dealt like a shuffled deck, so no place repeats until all 37 have been visited and two neighbouring stretches never share a look.
    • Real road surface. Procedural aggregate asphalt with a matching normal map, tyre-polished tracks, worn paint, black-and-yellow kerbs and cat's eyes that glow at night; it turns glossy in the rain.
    • Full day. Subah, dopehar, shaam and raat roll by on every run: lit windows, street lamps, neon, headlights, monsoon rain and Lahore smog.
    • Curved-world shader + fair spawner. A vertex bend makes the straight track sway over the horizon, and every obstacle has a fixed arrival distance, so an open lane is always guaranteed.

    How to Play

    • 🏍️ 125 ya 🛺 Rickshaw — menu ton sawari chuno
    • ←→ / AD / swipe — lane badlo (bike lean karti ae!)
    • ↑ W Space / swipe up — jump (khadde, barriers)
    • Hawa vich jump dobara — BACKFLIP (+score, +nitro)
    • Q X / 🏍️ — WHEELIE (125) ya DO PAHIYA (rickshaw do pahiyan te): jinna lamba, utne points te nitro
    • 😱 Gaddi de bilkul kol ton nikal — slow-motion te bonus!
    • 🏁 Sadak te TERA RECORD da gate — usnu cross kar ke record tod!
    • ↓ S / swipe down — brake, wheelie khatam, ya hawa ton slam down
    • Shift N / double-tap / ⚡ — Nitro: sab kuch tod de
    • H / tap — horn: agli gaddi side te ho jandi ae (truck, bhains te naka nahi!)
    • 🪁 Basant kites hawa vich latak rahiyan ne — jump ya ramp naal loot lo: BO KAATA!
    • 📞 Ammi da phone: dahi, naan, doodh ya anday chukk lo — dua milegi. Reh gaya ta chappal 🩴
    • Near misses, kites te wheelies naal combo x6 tak — fer 🔥 FEVER
    • Haath hilaundi sawari: kinare waali lane vich chukko, time te chhado
    • Power-ups: 🧲 Magnet · 🛡 Shield · 2x Coins · ⚡ Nitro · 🥛 Makhan Lassi
    • 🔤 L-A-H-O-R-E letters jama karo — roz da jackpot Rs 2500!
    • 🏍️ Billa Wheelie race lagaye ta usnu overtake karo
    • 📍 37 jagah, koi do vaari nahi — har gate te navi jagah unlock
    • 🌅 Menu ton waqt chuno: subah, dopehar, shaam ya raat
    • B Headstart · V Shield Start · R radio · P pause · M mute
