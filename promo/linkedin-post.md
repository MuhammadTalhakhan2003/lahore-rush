After Rikshaw Rush crossed 35,000 impressions, many of you asked one question: which city is next?

Lahore. 🏍️

I'm pleased to share Lahore Rush, the sequel. It's a 3D endless runner on a 125 through the city that never sleeps, and it runs right in your browser. No install, no download.

You start at Taxali Gate as the sun sets behind the Badshahi Masjid. Then the road takes you through Anarkali's bunting, past Minar-e-Pakistan, under the Orange Line on the Canal Road underpasses, and into MM Alam Road's neon at night. There are 37 real places, dealt like a shuffled deck, so no place repeats until you've seen them all. It's an endless ride from subah to raat.

Lahori life is everywhere:
🔹 A traffic warden flags you for overspeeding. Stop and pay half your earnings, or run and a police 125 chases you with siren and lights. If your pocket is empty, the challan goes on udhaar.
🔹 Ammi calls: "Puttar, wapsi te dahi lai aana." Bring it home for her dua, or the chappal is waiting.
🔹 Basant kites to loot, stray dogs and cats crossing ("billi rasta kaat gayi!"), tongas, chingchis, the red Metro Bus, and wheelies and backflips.

What I enjoyed engineering:
🔹 The 125 and its rider (shalwar kameez, waistcoat, aviators), Minar-e-Pakistan, the Badshahi Masjid, a tonga and the animals are all generated by a Python script in Blender.
🔹 The engine sound is fully synthesised. Every exhaust pulse is scheduled in real time at rpm/120, from the 11 Hz idle "dug-dug" to top gear. There's a 4-speed box with throttle cut, clutch, gear clunk and downshift blip, plus kick-start. No audio files.
🔹 Real road surfaces, black-and-yellow kerbs, cat's eyes that glow at night, and roads that turn glossy in the monsoon rain or vanish into smog.
🔹 A classic Lahori look: maroon, ivory and old gold, with a Mughal-arch emblem. It's responsive from small phones to tablets and desktops, and installable as an app.

Stack: Three.js · WebGL/GLSL · Web Audio · Blender (Python) · Vite · Vercel

As before, I built it with Claude Code as my AI pair-programmer. I made the design and product calls; the AI sped up the build.
